The added requirements cover every requested maintenance area.

- Every research release updates a cumulative handoff containing current and relevant prior research, derivations, computations and audits. Negative results, rejected approaches, superseded reviews and corrections retain their provenance and status.
- Conversational transcripts, credentials and unrelated private material stay out of the release handoff. Relevant arguments and audit objections become reviewable records. Required private evidence retains explicit access limitations and an unresolved objection cannot disappear through publication filtering.
- Current source and application inventories and the findings ledger are synchronized with the manuscript, PDF, README, artifact catalog and handoff. Version, date, source/build identity, title and status must agree.
- The archive index and release manifest record hashes, source commits, provenance, authorship, current/historical status, reproduction commands and tools, missing evidence and open work. Old editions remain at stable versioned locations. Manifest self-reference is handled with an external binding.
- Citation and credit changes, actual artifact representation and recoverability, inclusion requirements and release links receive explicit review.
- Configured public/private mirrors have defined content and permitted differences, authorized publication and readback against the reviewed manifest. Local success or one destination cannot establish the other destination's contents.
- Small non-release changes require a reasoned and reviewed not-applicable scope. An infrastructure or documentation label alone does not excuse maintenance obligations.
